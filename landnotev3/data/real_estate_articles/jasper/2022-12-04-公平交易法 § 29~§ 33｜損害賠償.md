> 損害賠償：今天要介紹在公平交易法中，當損害他人權益時，會面臨哪些賠償之相關規定。

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-b490971 latest_posts_content elementor-widget elementor-widget-theme-post-content" data-element_type="widget" data-id="b490971" data-widget_type="theme-post-content.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h1 class="wp-block-heading" id="土地增值稅重購退稅的意義">損害賠償之要件</h1>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．1. 權益受到保護</h3>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list" style="line-height:1.8">
<li> 當權益被侵害時，得請求除去之</li>
</ul>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list" style="line-height:1.8">
<li> 當權益有被侵害之虞時，得請求防止之</li>
</ul>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．2. 侵害他人權益者，應負損害賠償責任</h3>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．3. 賠償額的認定</h3>
<ul class="has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list" style="color:#030303;line-height:1.8">
<li>被侵害人得請求超過受損害之額度，但不能超過三倍</li>
</ul>
<ul class="has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list" style="color:#030303;line-height:1.8">
<li>侵害人若因侵害行為受有利益，被侵害人得就該利益計算賠償額度。</li>
</ul>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．4. 賠償請求權之消滅時效</h3>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list" style="line-height:1.8">
<li>被侵害人知侵害人或行為起：2年</li>
</ul>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list" style="line-height:1.8">
<li>若被侵害人不知，則行為開始後：10年</li>
</ul>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．5. 得請求將判決書登報</h3>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
