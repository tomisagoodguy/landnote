> 抵押權 vs 最高限額抵押權：抵押權是考試中很常出現的考點，今天就來簡單介紹抵押權和最高限額抵押權的差別

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-b490971 latest_posts_content elementor-widget elementor-widget-theme-post-content" data-element_type="widget" data-id="b490971" data-widget_type="theme-post-content.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h1 class="wp-block-heading" id="實例說明">抵押權 vs 最高限額抵押權的意義</h1>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size"><strong>抵押權：</strong>以不移轉佔有之不動產擔保某特定債權，對該不動產有優先受償之權</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:5px"></div>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size"><strong>最高限額抵押權：</strong>以不動產為擔保，就一定範圍內之不特定債權，設定抵押權</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:40px"></div>
<h1 class="wp-block-heading" id="實例說明">抵押權 vs 最高限額抵押權的特點</h1>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．相同處</h3>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size" style="line-height:1.8">兩者都是以不動產作為擔保</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:10px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．相異處</h3>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list">
<li class="has-normal-plus-font-size" style="line-height:1.8">擔保</li>
</ul>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size">抵押權：只能擔保已發生之債權</p>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size">最高限額抵押權：可擔保過去、現在和未來</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:5px"></div>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list">
<li>從屬性</li>
</ul>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size">抵押權：確定債權後才能設立，債權移轉(消滅)後隨之移轉（消滅）</p>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size">最高限額抵押權：可在無債權的情況設立，之後債權消滅仍能存在（範圍內之債權可不斷增減）</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:5px"></div>
<ul class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-block-list">
<li>擔保額度</li>
</ul>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size">抵押權：設立時已確定</p>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size">最高限額抵押權：在確定前額度都未定，經確定後轉為普通抵押權</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:40px"></div>
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<h5 class="wp-block-heading"><meta charset="utf-8"/></h5>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="hank-老師提醒">Ding 老師提醒</h2>
<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-normal-plus-font-size" style="line-height:1.8">實務上的最高限額抵押權會比貸款金額略高一點，因為銀行會在原本貸款額度上，多預估利息及違約金。</p>
