<h1 class="wp-block-heading">委任契約之定義</h1>
<p>稱委任者，謂當事人約定，一方委託他方處理事務，他方允為處理之契約。</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:40px"></div>
<h1 class="wp-block-heading">委任契約之要點</h1>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．委任性質</h3>
<p class="has-text-color has-link-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-elements-a2f093b40616e6e93de9315d0890fe5b" style="color:#000000;line-height:1.8">諾成、不要式之契約。</p>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．委任目的</h3>
<p class="has-text-color has-link-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-elements-4f58436ae637a24d3289a6da371415cc" style="color:#000000;line-height:1.8">委託他人處理特定事務。</p>
<ol class="wp-block-list" start="2"></ol>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．獲取報酬的依據</h3>
<p class="has-text-color has-link-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-elements-207353b4a241d1830c6664e03b10f80d" style="color:#000000;line-height:1.8">依照約定處理特定事務即可，不論該處理結果是否符合預期。</p>
<ol class="wp-block-list" start="3"></ol>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．從屬性低</h3>
<p class="has-text-color has-link-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-elements-1b5403e48aea9ba28924e2a32d13d68f" style="color:#000000;line-height:1.8">受委任者自由度高，可依自身的專業選擇處理方式。</p>
<ol class="wp-block-list" start="4"></ol>
<div aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer" style="height:20px"></div>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">．勞工法令</h3>
<p class="has-text-color has-link-color has-normal-plus-font-size wp-elements-5fc977389781c4f00ee97b756faa9380" style="color:#000000;line-height:1.8">視委任契約內容而定。</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Ding 老師提醒</h2>
